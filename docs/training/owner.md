# Owner guide

Everything a cashier and kitchen can do, plus **Setup**. Your PIN opens all your cafés; switch café at the top.

## Setting up
- **Cafés & kitchens**: add a café, its pickup letter and address, and its kitchen stations. Close a café for orders when needed.
- **Menu**: categories, items, prices (including GST), choices such as milk or size, and which kitchen station makes each item.
- **Staff**: add people, choose Cashier, Kitchen or Admin, the cafés they work at, and their PIN. Turn someone off when they leave.
- **Café settings**: name, welcome text, GST rate, and the details printed on receipts (legal name, GSTIN, FSSAI licence). With a GSTIN, receipts become tax invoices.
- **QR posters**: print one for each café so customers can scan and order.

## Every day
- **Reports**: sales by day and café, payment methods, top items, busiest hours, day closes, kitchen speed and the GST summary, for any dates.
- **Payment checks**: anything that needs a look, such as a refund that failed, a UPI payment without a reference, or an order paid but not served.
- **Inventory**: stock levels across cafés, low-stock warnings, and the stock report (received, used, wasted, counted).
- **Activity**: who changed what and when.

## For your accountant
On **Reports**, choose the dates and café and download: Orders, Items sold, Payments & refunds, Day closes, Stock movements and **GST invoices**. The GST summary on the same page gives totals by rate and the invoice numbers issued and cancelled.

## Kitchen speed
Set the minutes after which an order counts as late. The report shows typical times, late orders, and the hours when the kitchen is slowest, to help plan staff.

## Your plan
**Café settings** shows your plan and how many cafés and staff it allows. Contact Brew Haven to change plan.
