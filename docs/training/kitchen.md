# Kitchen guide

## Signing in
Tap your name and enter your 4-digit PIN. The app opens on the **Kitchen display**.

## Reading a ticket
Each ticket shows the pickup code, the customer's name, whether it came online or from the counter, and how many minutes since it was paid. Tickets turn red after 10 minutes. Choices (such as oat milk) and the customer's notes are shown under each item. If your café has more than one kitchen station, pick yours at the top to see only your items.

## Making an order
1. Tap **Start** when you begin. This tells the customer it's being made.
2. Tap **Ready** when it's done. The customer is told to collect it and the counter sees it.

Tap Start and Ready as you go, not in a batch: the owner's kitchen speed report uses these times.

## If an item has run out
For online orders, tap **Item ran out**, tick the missing items, add a short note if it helps, and tap **Ask customer**. Carry on with the rest. The customer picks a replacement, a refund for that item, or cancelling, and the ticket updates by itself.

If you can't make the order at all, tap **Reject** and give the reason the customer will see. They're refunded.

## Sold out
Switch the item off on **Stock** so nobody else can order it. Switch it back on when it's back.

## If the screen says "Reconnecting"
The display still refreshes every 20 seconds. If the internet is down, the counter will call orders through to you; they appear on the screen once the connection is back.
