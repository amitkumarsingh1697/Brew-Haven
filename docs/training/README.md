# Staff training

One short guide per role. The same text opens from the **Help** button at the top of the staff app,
showing the guide for whoever is signed in, so changes here reach the app on the next build.

- [cashier.md](cashier.md): counter staff
- [kitchen.md](kitchen.md): kitchen and barista staff
- [owner.md](owner.md): the café owner (Admin)

A first session takes about 20 minutes per person: sign in, take two practice counter orders
(one cash, one UPI), mark one ready on the kitchen screen, hand it over, print a receipt, and
close the day. Use a practice café or do it before opening and reject the practice orders.
