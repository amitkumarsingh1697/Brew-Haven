# Cashier guide

## Signing in
Tap your name, then enter your 4-digit PIN. Keep your PIN to yourself: everything you do is recorded under your name. Sign out when you leave the till.

## The screens you use
- **Orders**: every order for your café, newest first, live. A number on the tab shows orders waiting to start.
- **New counter order**: take an order at the counter.
- **Kitchen display**: the same tickets the kitchen sees.
- **Stock**: mark items sold out, and record deliveries, waste and shelf counts.
- **Today**: today's takings and closing the day.

## Taking a counter order
1. Open **New counter order** and tap the items. Items with choices (such as milk) ask which one.
2. Use − and + to change quantities. Add the customer's name if you like, so you can call it out.
3. **Take the payment first.** Then tap how they paid: **Cash**, **UPI** or **Card**.
4. For UPI, type the 12-digit UPI reference (UTR) from the customer's screen if you can. Brew Haven refuses a reference that was already used, so one payment can't pay for two orders.
5. The order goes straight to the kitchen and you get its pickup code. Tap **Print receipt** if the customer wants one.

## When the internet is down
A red banner says you're offline. You can still take counter orders: the till saves each one as **OFF-1**, **OFF-2** and so on, and tells you what to call through to the kitchen, because the kitchen screen can't see it yet. When the connection is back, the till sends them by itself and shows which pickup code each one became. If one says **Not accepted**, read the reason, then **Try again** or **Remove** it (only remove it if you've refunded the customer or entered it again). Don't clear the browser's data while orders are waiting.

## Handing over
When the kitchen marks an order **Ready**, it shows on Orders. Check the pickup code with the customer, then tap **Handed over**.

## Problems with an order
- **Reject**: if you can't make an order at all. Type the reason the customer will see. Online payments are refunded automatically. For counter orders, hand the cash back; Brew Haven records it as a manual refund.
- **Item ran out** (online orders): tick the missing items and tap **Ask customer**. The customer chooses a replacement, removal with refund, or cancelling. The ticket shows "waiting on customer" until they answer.

## Sold out and stock
On **Stock**, switch an item off when it runs out so customers can't order it, and back on when it's back. Record deliveries, waste and shelf counts for ingredients there too.

## Closing the day
On **Today**, count the cash in the drawer and enter the opening float and the amount counted. Brew Haven shows what should be there and whether you're over or short. Add a note if something explains a difference. Once closed, the day's figures are kept for the owner.
