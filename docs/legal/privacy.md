# Privacy policy

*For people who order at a café that uses Brew Haven, and for café staff and owners. Last updated [date].*

## Who is responsible for your data
- **If you order at a café:** the café you order from decides how your details are used. It is the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Brew Haven, run by [Inferra Software legal name], processes your details for the café. Questions about a café's use of your data go to that café first; we will help them answer.
- **If you own a café account:** we are the data fiduciary for your own sign-up and billing details.

## What we hold
| Who | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | Name, mobile number, order and pickup details, notes you type | To make and hand over your order and tell you when it is ready |
| Customers | Whether you agreed to WhatsApp updates or offers, and when | To send only the messages you agreed to |
| Customers | Payment status and reference from the payment gateway | To confirm payment and handle refunds. We do not see or store card numbers or UPI PINs |
| Customers | Wallet balance and loyalty, if the café uses them | To keep your balance |
| Staff | Name, role, cafés you work at, sign-in PIN (stored only as a one-way hash) | To let you sign in and record who did what |
| Owners | Name, email, mobile, business name and address, GSTIN | To run the account, bill and support you |
| Everyone | Activity log, IP address, device and browser details | Security, preventing fraud, and fixing faults |
| Customers | Which ordering steps a visit reached (opened, menu, added, checkout, paying), under a random id kept only in that browser tab and not linked to you. Not collected if your browser asks not to be tracked | To show the café how many visits turn into orders |

We use your details only for the purposes above. When you place an order, you give your details so the café can make it and tell you when it is ready. Offers and marketing need your separate consent.

## Messages
Order updates by WhatsApp or SMS are sent only if you give your number for that order. Offers and marketing are sent only if you separately agree, and you can stop them at any time by replying STOP or asking the café. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and does not affect messages already sent.

## Who we share with
Only service providers we need to run Brew Haven:
- Render (runs the app) and Neon on Amazon Web Services (stores the data), both in Singapore.
- Cloudflare, which delivers the app to your phone and protects it from attacks.
- The café's payment gateway (Razorpay), in India.
- Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, if the café sends order updates on WhatsApp.

Each is bound by a contract to protect the data. We do not sell personal data and do not show advertising. We share data with authorities only when Indian law requires it.

## Where data is stored
In Singapore, which is not a country restricted by the Government of India under section 16 of the DPDP Act. Card and UPI details never reach us; they stay with the payment gateway in India.

## How long we keep it
- Order records, including customer name and number: 8 years, because cafés must keep tax and accounting records (GST and income tax law).
- Marketing consent records: while consent lasts, plus 3 years as proof.
- Staff accounts: until removed by the café, then 90 days. Names on past orders and the activity log stay with those records.
- Security logs: 1 year, as the DPDP Rules require.
- When a café closes its account, its data is deleted as set out in the Terms of service, except what the law requires to be kept.

## Your rights
Under the DPDP Act you can ask to see a summary of your data and who it was shared with, correct or update it, have it erased when it is no longer needed (except what the law requires us to keep), withdraw consent, and nominate someone to act for you if you die or cannot act. Ask the café, or write to our grievance officer. We will reply within 30 days.

## Children
If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place the order for you. Brew Haven does not knowingly collect children's details, track children or send them offers. Café staff accounts are for adults only.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, PINs are stored only as hashes, each café business can see only its own data, and staff see only what their role allows. If a breach affects your data, we and the café will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
