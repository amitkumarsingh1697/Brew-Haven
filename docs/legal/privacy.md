# Privacy policy

*For people who order at a café that uses Brew Haven, and for café staff and owners. Last updated [date].*

## Who is responsible for your data
- **If you order at a café:** the café you order from decides how your details are used. It is the data fiduciary under the DPDP Act, 2023. Brew Haven, run by [Inferra Software legal name], processes your details for the café. Questions about a café's use of your data go to that café first; we will help them answer.
- **If you own a café account:** we are the data fiduciary for your own sign-up and billing details.

## What we hold
| Who | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | Name, mobile number, order and pickup details, notes you type | To make and hand over your order and tell you when it is ready |
| Customers | Whether you agreed to WhatsApp updates or offers, and when | To send only the messages you agreed to |
| Customers | Payment status and reference from the payment gateway | To confirm payment and handle refunds. We do not see or store card numbers or UPI PINs |
| Customers | Wallet balance and loyalty, if the café uses them | To keep your balance |
| Staff | Name, role, cafés you work at, sign-in PIN (stored only as a one-way hash) | To let you sign in and record who did what |
| Owners | Name, email, mobile, business name and address | To run the account, bill and support you |
| Everyone | Activity log, IP address, device and browser details | Security, preventing fraud, and fixing faults |

## Messages
Order updates by WhatsApp or SMS are sent only if you give your number for that order. Offers and marketing are sent only if you separately agree, and you can stop them at any time by replying STOP or asking the café.

## Who we share with
Only service providers we need to run Brew Haven: hosting and database ([providers, regions]), the café's payment gateway, and the messaging provider for WhatsApp or SMS ([provider]). Each is bound by a contract to protect the data. We do not sell personal data and do not show advertising. We share data with authorities only when Indian law requires it.

## Your rights
Under the DPDP Act you can ask to see a summary of your data, correct or update it, have it erased when it is no longer needed, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to act for you. Ask the café, or write to our grievance officer. We will reply within [30] days.

## Children
Brew Haven is not meant for children to sign up. Café staff accounts are for adults only.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit, PINs are stored only as hashes, each café business can see only its own data, and staff see only what their role allows. If a breach affects your data, we and the café will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
