# Legal pages

`terms.md` and `privacy.md` are what the customer app shows at `/legal/terms` and `/legal/privacy`,
and what new businesses accept at sign-up. They are **drafts for lawyer review** (12 Nov 2026) and
show a "Draft" notice until the build sets `VITE_LEGAL_FINAL=true`.

`refund-and-cancellation-policy.md` is the refund and cancellation policy for 400 Coffee, with the edge cases and research behind it (13 Nov 2026). It is not yet shown in the app.

To change the text, edit these files; the pages pick them up on the next build. Placeholders are in
[square brackets]. The reviewer's open points are in the shared draft document, not here.
